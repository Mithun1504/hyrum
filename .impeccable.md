## Design Context

### Users

Platform and API team leads are the primary users, with API support, developer experience, and SRE teams reviewing the same evidence. They use Hyrum during focused change reviews to decide whether a proposed API change is safe, who it affects, and what proof supports the decision.

### Brand Personality

Calm, forensic, decisive. The product speaks precisely, separates fact from inference, and never dramatizes routine risk.

### Aesthetic Direction

A dark industrial product interface built around evidence, sequence, and decision provenance. Dense enough for expert investigation, but ordered enough that the next action is obvious. Avoid neon cyberpunk styling, generic analytics dashboards, decorative charts, glass effects, and visual noise.

### Design Principles

- Put deterministic evidence and receipts ahead of summaries or generated prose.
- Make T1 block, T2 warn, and T3 exposure visibly distinct without relying on color alone.
- Keep the primary decision path obvious: inspect the changed behavior, verify the receipt, then pin or resolve it.
- Use progressive disclosure for raw technical detail; never hide facts needed to audit a gate decision.
- Preserve full functionality on phones and tablets with touch-safe controls and reflowed evidence views.
- Treat loading, empty, unavailable, stale, flaky, and permission-limited states as first-class product states.
